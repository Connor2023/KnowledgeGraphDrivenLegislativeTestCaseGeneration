Two conventions to follow in every answer below.

Subjects: the subject of an obligation or permission is always the regulated party -- 'APP entity', 'organisation', 'agency' or 'individual'. Never make an abstract noun such as 'steps', 'use or disclosure', 'principles' or 'the information' the subject of an obligation. Where a provision limits or excludes itself, the subject is the subclause or the principle.

Purposes: for 'may use for', 'must not use for' and 'must not disclose for', the object is the purpose, not the information acted on. Record the information in its own separate triple.
