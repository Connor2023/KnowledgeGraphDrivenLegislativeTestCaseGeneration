Under Australian Privacy Principle 2 (anonymity and pseudonymity), subclause 2.2 of Schedule 1 of the Privacy Act provides that subclause 2.1 does not apply if, in relation to that matter the APP entity is required or authorised by or under an Australian law, or a court/tribunal order, to deal with individuals who have identified themselves; or it is impracticable for the APP entity to deal with individuals who have not identified themselves or who have used a pseudonym.
